Service Introduction

Yearling Prediction is a service that helps auction buyers predict the future performance of yearlings based on their pedigree and physical traits. Buyers can offer us expected budget and preferred characteristics, and we will provide a list of recommended yearlings that best match their preferences.

ⓘ MVP Data Declaration: All the data for this MVP comes from Silk Racing 2025 yearlings and Magic Millions Gold Coast 2026 yearling auction, and is only used here for MVP demonstration.
