Ace Breeding

Service Introduction

For new stallions, we help predict their breeding characteristics and suitable mares. Especially for stallions without progeny records, we can predict their potential offspring's characteristics based on their pedigree and physical traits, helping breeders find suitable mares and make informed breeding decisions.

Potential breeding outcomes will be judged both from pedigree and physical traits.

The nicks will be predicted based on Machine Learning results.

Breeding Prediction for New Stallions (Prototype)

Suggestions:

For new stallions without progeny records, we can predict their breeding performance and competitiveness, especially in high COI environments. If possible, we will encourage breeders to make them as shuttle stallions to different areas.

Report fee: 0.1% of the total yearly breeding fee.

What we hope to bring?
  • Best breeding result for every mare
  • Save the time to find nicks for stallions
  • Help buyers find the most suitable yearlings under budget
