Introduction

Ace Breeding is an AI-motivated start-up project, aiming at predicting future performances of thoroughbred horses using machine learning, saving the time and efforts required to breed or find a true good horse.

Our Service includes:

Stallion Matching: Based on your mare's characteristics and your expected price, find the most suitable stallion.

Breeding Prediction: For new stallions, we help predict their breeding characteristics and suitable mares.

Yearling Prediction: For yearlings, we predict their future performances and give suggestions to auction buyers.
